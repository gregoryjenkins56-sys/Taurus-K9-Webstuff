How it works

Four steps from first call to a trained dog

No guesswork and no surprise invoices. You always know which stage you are in and what comes next.

  1. Free meet and greet

    In person, no cost, no commitment. We assess temperament, environment and goals, then tell you what is realistic.

  2. A written plan and quote

    You get the recommended program, the length, any training tools, and the cost in writing before anything starts.

  3. Training with daily updates

    Board and Train runs multiple structured sessions daily across varied settings. Lessons run in your home and neighborhoods.

  4. Handoff and follow-through

    We hand you the handling skills, not just a trained dog, plus a maintenance plan and trainer support.

Why clients choose us

What certified actually means here

Anyone can call themselves a dog trainer. These are the credentials you can verify before you hand over your dog.

Temperament-first assessment

Our approach centers on understanding the individual temperament of each dog and building a strategy rooted in scientific principles, not a template applied to every dog.

Low-stress, motivational methods

We use motivational techniques so your dog learns with minimal stress. A dog that is not shut down learns faster and keeps the behavior longer.

Real-world proofing

Skills are trained in varied settings: home, parks, parking lots and public spaces, so reliability survives contact with the real world.

You are trained too

A dog that is only obedient for the trainer is not trained. Handoff includes handler education so the results stay yours.
